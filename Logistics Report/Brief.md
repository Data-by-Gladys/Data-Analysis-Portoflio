# Logistics Operations Analysis - Fleet, Driver, Route & Customer Performance

## Industry: Logistics & Transportation

## Tools: Power BI (Power Query, Data Modelling, DAX, Interactive Dashboards)

## Data Source: Logistics Operations Database

### 1. Introduction

This project analyses logistics operations across customers, loads, trips, drivers, trucks, trailers, routes, fuel purchases, maintenance activities, delivery events, and safety incidents. The objective is to understand overall operational performance, fleet and driver efficiency, route and customer contribution, fuel consumption, maintenance activity, delivery performance, and safety-related costs.

The analysis brings together operational and financial data to provide a clearer view of how transportation resources are being used and where management can focus attention to improve efficiency, service delivery, and cost control.

### 2. Objectives

The analysis focuses on three major areas:

**Operational Overview:** Understanding overall revenue, customer activity, load volume, booking patterns, and delivery performance.

**Fleet & Driver Performance:** Evaluating driver performance, truck utilization, fuel efficiency, maintenance activity, and fleet performance trends.

**Route, Customer & Cost Efficiency:** Examining route revenue, customer contribution, fuel efficiency, maintenance costs, vehicle and cargo damage, accident claims, and operational cost patterns.

### 3. Key Metrics

The dashboard provides answers to several key analytical questions, including:

a. How is the logistics operation performing in terms of revenue, loads, customers, and trips?

b. How does revenue and trip activity change over time?

c. Which customers and customer types contribute the most revenue?

d. How efficiently are drivers and trucks performing in terms of on-time delivery, fuel efficiency, utilization, and revenue generation?

e. Which trucks have higher maintenance activity and how does this relate to fleet utilization?

f. Which routes generate the strongest revenue and revenue yield?

g. How does fuel consumption and cost vary across operations?

h. What types of safety incidents occur and what financial impact do vehicle damage and claims have?

### 4. Dataset Overview & Methodology

The Logistics Operations Database contains 14 related datasets covering different areas of transportation operations. The tables include customers, drivers, trucks, trailers, facilities, routes, loads, trips, fuel purchases, maintenance records, delivery events, safety incidents, driver monthly metrics, and truck utilization metrics.

The database contains both transactional and aggregated operational data. Key relationships connect customers to loads, loads to routes and trips, trips to drivers, trucks and trailers, and trips to fuel purchases, delivery events, and safety information. This relational structure makes it possible to analyse operational performance from multiple perspectives.

### 5. Key Findings

The dashboard highlights several important patterns across the logistics operation. The business recorded ₦257.26M in total revenue across 85.41K loads and 200 customers, indicating a large operational volume. Revenue varies across booking types and customer types, with some customer segments contributing more significantly to overall revenue than others.

Fleet performance shows an average fuel efficiency of 6.50 MPG, while truck utilization and maintenance activity vary across the fleet. The truck breakdown trend also changes over time, highlighting the importance of monitoring vehicle reliability and downtime.

Driver performance analysis shows differences in on-time delivery rates and average MPG across drivers. This suggests that driver-level performance monitoring can help identify areas for additional training, recognition, or operational support.

Route and cost analysis highlights differences in revenue yield, fuel efficiency, maintenance labour costs, and vehicle and cargo damage costs across operations. The safety analysis also shows variation in incident types and claim amounts, providing useful information for monitoring accident-related financial exposure and preventable incidents.

Overall, the findings show that revenue alone does not provide a complete picture of logistics performance. Fuel efficiency, fleet utilization, maintenance, delivery performance, customer contribution, and safety costs need to be considered together when evaluating operational efficiency.

### 6. Conclusion

This project provides an interactive view of logistics operations across revenue, customers, loads, drivers, trucks, routes, fuel, maintenance, deliveries, and safety incidents. The three dashboards transform the underlying logistics database into a practical analytical tool for monitoring both financial and operational performance.

The analysis demonstrates how Power BI, Power Query, data modelling, and DAX can be used to turn complex relational data into meaningful business insights. By monitoring revenue alongside fuel efficiency, fleet utilization, maintenance activity, driver performance, customer contribution, delivery performance, and safety incidents, the business can gain a more complete understanding of its operations and identify areas for improving efficiency, service quality, and cost management.

Power BI was used for data preparation, modelling, DAX calculations, and interactive dashboard development. Power Query was used to prepare the datasets and ensure fields were correctly structured for analysis. Relationships between the operational tables were established using primary and foreign keys, while calculated measures were created to evaluate revenue, delivery performance, fuel efficiency, utilization, maintenance costs, and other operational KPIs.
